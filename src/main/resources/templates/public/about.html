About JobConnect

We're building the most transparent way to hire and get hired.

Job searching shouldn't feel like sending applications into the void. We make every step visible — for candidates and for the teams hiring them.

Our mission

Make hiring fair, fast and human.

JobConnect started with a simple frustration: great candidates were losing out because they never heard back, and great teams were losing candidates because their process was opaque.

Today we help companies publish clear, salary-transparent roles and give every applicant a live view of where they stand. Better information on both sides leads to better matches.

  • Salary ranges shown on every listing
  • Every application tracked from submission to decision
  • Employers verified and every posting moderated

The principles behind the product

Transparency first

Respect for candidates

Privacy policy

We collect only what's needed to run the service: your account details, profile, resumes you upload and applications you submit.

  • Your resume is shared only with employers you apply to.
  • We never sell personal data to third parties.
  • You can update or delete your profile and resumes at any time.

Terms of service

By using JobConnect you agree to provide accurate information and to use the platform for genuine hiring and job-seeking purposes.

  • Employers must post real, currently open positions.
  • Discriminatory or misleading postings are removed.
  • Accounts that violate these terms may be suspended.
